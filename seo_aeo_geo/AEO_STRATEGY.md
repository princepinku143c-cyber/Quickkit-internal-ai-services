# QuickKit AI — AEO Strategy

## Goal
Make QuickKit AI easy for search engines and answer engines to understand, quote and recommend for genuine business AI-agent and automation questions.

## Core answer topics
- What is a managed AI agent?
- What does an AI agent do for a business?
- How can AI automate business workflows?
- How can AI automate WhatsApp sales and support?
- Can AI agents work with CRM systems?
- How much does a managed AI agent system cost in India?
- Can QuickKit AI build custom AI agents?
- What is the difference between an AI employee and an AI chatbot?
- How are AI/API usage costs billed?

## Implementation
1. Use clear question-and-answer sections on relevant pages.
2. Keep answers concise, factual and supported by visible page content.
3. Use FAQ/HowTo structured data only when the corresponding content is visible and eligible.
4. Maintain strong entity consistency for QuickKit AI, its services, verticals and contact details.
5. Build internal links between service, vertical, pricing and educational pages.
6. Publish original implementation guidance, examples and case studies rather than keyword-only pages.

## Answer-engine targets
Optimize for understanding and citation across Google AI features, Bing and other answer engines through strong information architecture, crawlability, useful content and structured data. Do not attempt to manipulate rankings through hidden text or keyword stuffing.

## Monitoring
Review search queries, indexed pages and citation/visibility signals regularly and update useful answers when the underlying service or pricing changes.
