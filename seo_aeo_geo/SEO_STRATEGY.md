# QuickKit AI — SEO Strategy for quickkitai.com

## Positioning
Primary market: businesses in India looking for managed AI agents, AI automation, AI employees and custom business workflow automation.

Real estate is a priority vertical, not the entire brand positioning.

## Technical SEO
1. Unique title and meta description per indexable page.
2. One clear search-intent-focused H1 per page.
3. Canonical URLs and crawlable internal links.
4. XML sitemap and robots.txt kept current.
5. Service and FAQ structured data only where the visible page supports it.
6. Fast loading, responsive mobile UX and optimized assets.
7. No keyword stuffing or doorway pages.

## Content strategy
Publish useful service pages, vertical pages, implementation guides, comparisons and real case studies. Each page should target one primary intent and a small set of closely related terms.

## Local / GEO strategy
Use city-specific pages only when QuickKit AI has a genuine service offer or useful localized content for that city. Do not mass-generate city keyword pages.

## Measurement
Track impressions, clicks, indexed pages, conversions and query groups in Search Console and the existing rank-tracking workflow.
