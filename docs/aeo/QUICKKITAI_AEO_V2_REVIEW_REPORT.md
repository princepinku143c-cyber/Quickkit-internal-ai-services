## Measurement rules
- `run_status` and `outcome` are separate fields.
- ERROR, BLOCKED and NOT_RUN must never be counted as ABSENT.
- Mention, recommendation and citation are separate signals.
- Recommendation requires explicit recommendation language and a saved answer excerpt.
- Citation requires a captured URL; verify the URL and classify whether it is an official domain.
- Every rate must include numerator, denominator, platform and observation window.
- Manual browser runs must preserve the exact prompt, timestamp, locale, captured answer and evidence reference.
- Do not claim rankings, citations, traffic, leads, bookings or revenue from inventory readiness.

## Review findings and remaining gates
- This is a complete V2 draft inventory, not an owner-approved final set.
- Branded prompts test what can be independently verified about QuickKitAI; they do not assume positive results.
- Competitor prompts compare categories fairly. Named competitor benchmarking remains blocked until an owner-approved source-backed register exists.
- The schema is structurally drafted. It has not yet been proven by storing real observations from ChatGPT, Gemini or Perplexity.
- A small manual baseline can be run only by capturing actual platform responses; no such response is fabricated in this pack.
- No production website, VPS, Firebase, WhatsApp, QR code, or main branch change is included.
