# RAJA AI SYSTEMS — PUBLIC WEBSITE SOURCE OF TRUTH

## Positioning
Raja AI Systems is a B2B autonomous AI agency for high-ticket real estate and healthcare. The core product is a five-member autonomous AI workforce with WhatsApp-first customer interaction and managed deployment/operations.

## Commercial offer
Regular anchor: ₹70,000 implementation setup + ₹2,40,000/month recurring retainer.

Dussehra pilot offer: ₹35,000 upfront setup + ₹1,20,000/month management for up to 3 pilot clients. The supplied master ledger describes the discounted retainer as locked for life under the agreed contract.

The retainer is described as starting on Day 30 after launch/result delivery in the supplied sales script. Exact payment, qualification and refund terms are governed by the signed agreement.

## Performance SLA
Real estate: 10 qualified physical site visits within 30 days.
Healthcare: 15 qualified in-clinic consultations within 30 days.
The supplied ledger states a 100% refund of the ₹35,000 setup fee if the applicable target is not achieved, subject to the written qualifying conditions.

## The five-member workforce
1. 12-Second WhatsApp Speed-to-Lead Closer — inbound qualifier and appointment booker using WhatsApp voice-note + text.
2. Apify B2B Lead Prospector + Competitor Intelligence — targeted prospecting, public business/ad research, executive dossiers and a max 50-prospect/day throttle.
3. CRM Sentinel + No-Show Guard — appointment handling, calendar lock, pre-visit location reminder and transportation workflow.
4. Dead Lead Reviver — reactivation of dormant lead databases with short conversational hooks.
5. 5-Star Reputation + Local SEO Sentinel — positive-review routing, unhappy-lead escalation and local/AI-search visibility support.

## Primary workflow
Traffic / enquiry → 12-second WhatsApp response → qualification → appointment/site-visit → reminder/proof → CRM/human sales handoff → dormant-lead reactivation → reputation follow-up.

## Advertising message
The supplied 60-second campaign blueprint is:
pain/hook → late-night lead problem → five-agent live proof → 10-visit performance SLA → Dussehra price anchor → direct WhatsApp CTA.

Primary ad destination: direct WhatsApp click-to-chat.

## Guardrails
Public website copy must distinguish marketing positioning from account-level proof. A feature is not represented as live merely because it is technically possible.

Do not publish fabricated testimonials, fabricated integrations, guaranteed search rankings or guaranteed ROI.

The site should state when an external provider, client account, credentials, traffic volume, message templates or other dependency is required.

## Brand rule
Public landing-page branding is Raja AI Systems. The existing QuickKit internal/app naming is retained only where required for backwards compatibility in the codebase.

## Pricing rule
Do not reintroduce the legacy public KVM 4 / KVM 8 marketing prices into the Raja AI Systems landing page. Those constants remain only for backwards compatibility with internal code paths.
